What to Discuss Before Installation

The exact work depends on the building and the roof. Use your first conversation to find out what is included and what remains outside the proposed scope. Helpful questions include:

  • Which roof areas and components are part of the work?
  • Does the plan account for the condition of the structure beneath the roof covering?
  • How will you be told about any added work that may be needed?
  • What should be moved or kept clear before the work starts?
  • Who should you contact if you have a concern during the project?

Have the property address, your observations, and any relevant notes ready. If there are signs of a leak, describe the location and timing rather than guessing at the cause. Avoid choosing a plan based only on a brief description or assuming that every roof requires the same materials and preparation. The contractor should explain how the recommendation fits your home.
