What to note before arranging a visit

A short record of what has happened can be more helpful than a guess about the cause. Before you call, write down:

  • When you first noticed the change and whether it has returned.
  • Whether the affected area seems larger or different now.
  • What you can see from inside, without opening walls or entering roof spaces.
