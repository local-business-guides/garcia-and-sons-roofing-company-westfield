What to have ready before a roof assessment

A useful first conversation starts with details you already have. You do not need to diagnose the roof yourself. Gather a short record of the concern and prepare to describe the building and access arrangements.

  • Note the date you first noticed the issue and whether it has happened more than once.
  • Identify the rooms or work areas involved, without entering an unsafe area.
  • Take photos from a safe indoor location if they help show what you observed.
  • Share any prior roof paperwork or repair notes that are available.
  • Ask who should be present and what access the contractor may need.

Avoid climbing onto the roof, applying a patch without understanding the source, or treating an interior mark as proof of a particular roof defect. Those steps can create safety risks or leave the original concern unresolved. Commercial Roofing Westfield decisions are easier to discuss when the observations are clear and the contractor can explain what has and has not been established.
