What to prepare and what to ask

A little preparation can make a roof assessment more productive. Before the visit, consider gathering:

  • The location of any interior discoloration or moisture, including the room and ceiling or wall area.
  • When you first noticed it, and whether it has appeared more than once.
  • Photos you’ve already taken from a safe place indoors.
  • Any prior observations about the same part of the property.

You can also ask how the contractor plans to trace the source, what areas may need access, and how you’ll receive an explanation of the results. Avoid climbing onto the roof or making a repair based on a guess. A patch in the wrong place may not address the source, and roof access can be hazardous. If water is actively entering or the concern is spreading, contact a professional promptly rather than waiting for more signs.
